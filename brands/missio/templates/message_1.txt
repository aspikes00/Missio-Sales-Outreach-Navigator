STRUCTURE: Brief warm open → specific observation about their role/company/post → one-sentence implication (why it matters) → ONE binary or permission question with Sandler out. No product mention, no CTA.

EXEMPLAR (write a fresh version using real profile data — do not copy this):
{first_name}, appreciate the connection.

{one specific observation — something from their recent post, headline, hiring trend, company milestone, or role context. Be concrete enough that only this person could have sent it.} {one sentence on why this observation is interesting — the implied challenge or opportunity it signals for their marketing work. Not a pain probe — just a relevant inference.}

Is {relevant topic — something tied to the observation} something worth a quick conversation, or is the timing not right?

KEY RULES:
- The observation must be real and specific. If their recent post is available, reference a specific idea or line from it — not just "I saw your post." If no posts are available, use their headline, role, or company context.
- The implication is one sentence — a logical inference about what the observation means for their work. Not a pain diagnosis. More like: "That kind of growth usually creates real pressure on the attribution side."
- The question must be binary or permission-based — yes/no, or an easy single-word answer. Never open-ended ("what are your challenges?"), never multiple choice, never compound.
- "You" / "your" must outnumber "I" / "we" / "my" / "our" in the message. Count them.
- Zero product mention, zero value prop, zero calculator URL. This message is about them, not Missio.
- Sandler close built into the question: "or is the timing not right?" — always include a soft out.
- Tone: two marketing leaders talking, not a vendor following up a connection request.
- Keep it under 150 words. Shorter is better here.
